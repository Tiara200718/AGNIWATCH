SENSOR SPECIFICATION & RESOLUTION MATRIX

MODIS (Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer)
Platforms: Terra (10:30 AM/PM), Aqua (1:30 AM/PM)
Pixel Resolution: 1 km at nadir (expands at scan edge)
Bands: 4 µm and 11 µm brightness temperature channels
Confidence: Algorithmic probability score (0–100%)
VIIRS (Visible Infrared Imaging Radiometer Suite)
Platforms: Suomi-NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21
Pixel Resolution: 375 m at nadir (higher spatial fidelity)
Bands: I-Band (I4: 3.74 µm) & M-Band thermal channels
Confidence: Categorical (low, nominal, high)
⚠ THERMAL ANOMALY vs GROUND FIRE PERIMETER

Hotspots detected by NASA FIRMS indicate elevated radiance at sensor pixel scale (1 km for MODIS, 375 m for VIIRS). They are thermal anomalies, not surveyed ground fire perimeters or burn scars.

◎ SENSOR HARMONIZATION STATUS

MODIS and VIIRS have different spatial resolutions, swath geometries, saturation thresholds, and overpass schedules. Simply merging observation counts does not constitute scientific harmonization. Cross-sensor comparison requires spatial resampling and temporal normalization.

☁ CLOUD & CANOPY OBSCURATION

Heavy clouds, thick convective smoke, or dense forest canopies can mask thermal signatures, preventing orbital detection. Absence of detections does not verify absence of ground burning.
